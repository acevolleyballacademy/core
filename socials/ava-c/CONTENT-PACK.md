## 8. Lowlight Reel template (every match)

1. **Ask the player first** in the group chat ("ok to post? 😂"). No answer = don't post.
2. The fail at normal speed → freeze frame → zoom in → sound effect (CapCut has "bruh", record scratch, sad trombone in its effects library).
3. Overlay `assets/overlay_lowlight.png`.

**Caption bank:**
- `Lowlight of the week goes to… 🏆` / `Lowlight týdne vyhrává… 🏆`
